Privacy Policy

Run & Jump Adventure
Developer: READY COM
Android package: com.cloudrun.cloudhop
Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how Run & Jump Adventure handles information when you play the game or interact with advertisements. For privacy questions, contact kolonil97@outlook.com.

1. Game data stored on your device

The game stores coins, unlocked levels and characters, best scores, completion times, stars, cosmetic selections, and music, sound and graphics preferences on your device. This information is used to remember your progress and settings. The current game code does not send this saved progress to a developer-operated server.

You do not need an account, and the game does not ask for your name, phone number or email address to play.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The app includes advertising. Rewarded videos are optional: you choose whether to watch an advertisement for the reward shown in the game.

Depending on the advertising networks enabled in the released app, advertisements may be provided by Google AdMob and by Unity LevelPlay mediation with ironSource Ads, Unity Ads and Meta Audience Network. Not every provider serves every advertisement.

Advertising providers may collect or receive advertising or device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, device and operating-system information, app information, advertisement views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Processing depends on the provider, SDK configuration, device settings and applicable consent choices.

Providers use this information to deliver and measure advertisements, limit repeated advertisements, detect fraud, maintain their services and, where permitted, personalize advertising. Their policies explain their processing and sharing practices:

  • Google Privacy Policy and Google services in partner apps
  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy (Unity Ads, LevelPlay and ironSource services)
  • Meta Privacy Policy

The gameplay features do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise GPS location.

3. Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use it to respond to your request and resolve support or privacy issues. Please do not send unnecessary sensitive information.

4. Sharing and international processing

Advertising requests transmit information to the relevant advertising providers. These providers may process information with their service providers and advertising partners, including in countries other than your own, as described in their privacy policies. Support messages may be processed by our email service provider. We may disclose information when required by law or in response to a valid legal request.

5. Retention and deletion

Saved game data remains in the app's local storage until you clear the app's storage or remove the app. Android backup settings may preserve or restore app data separately. Clearing app storage deletes your local progress, coins and settings.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to address your request and applicable legal obligations. Advertising providers apply their own retention periods. Uninstalling the game does not automatically delete information already held by an advertising provider. Consult the provider's privacy controls or contact it to request deletion.

6. Privacy choices and rights

You can decline optional rewarded videos. You can manage available advertising-ID and advertising privacy controls in Android settings and use privacy controls offered by advertising providers. Declining a rewarded video does not necessarily prevent other advertising requests when other ad formats are enabled.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to or restrict processing, obtain a copy of information, or withdraw consent. Contact us for requests concerning information we hold. For information held by advertising providers, follow the instructions in their privacy policies. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

7. Children's privacy

Run & Jump Adventure is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app or a support request, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion of information within our control.

The age at which a user can independently consent to certain data processing may be higher than 13 in some locations. The game's intended audience does not replace applicable consent requirements.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information within our control. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Advertising providers describe their safeguards in their policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. This page will show the date of the latest update.
